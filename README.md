# TizenTube userscript (Erik's build)

`u.js` is the TizenTube userscript (@foxreis/tizentube 1.15.2) with two additions, loaded by a personal build of
TizenTube Cobalt for Android TV:

- **AI Content Filter**: Enable (AI videos don't play, a message says why) and Complete block (also hidden from feeds
  and search). Lists: [AiSList](https://github.com/Override92/AiSList) and [Soul Over AI](https://github.com/xoundbyte/soul-over-ai).
- **Force Music Only**: a music video plays as its song from YouTube Music.

Generated file; not meant for other use.
